## Software baselines

FastMACE 1.0 combines whole-step CUDA Graphs, exact model rewrites, cuEq/OEq and structure-specific backend selection. The authors report 3.8–10× lower per-step time than TorchSim on their RTX 5060 Ti FP32 benchmarks (216–1,944 atoms), including 2.90 ms/step for 216-atom NaCl with MACE-MH-1. These are the software authors’ measurements. The supported 1.0 stack and test precision differ from our V100 FP64 runs. [Release and benchmark](https://pypi.org/project/fastmace/1.0.0/)

The native MACE calculator constructs a base batch before looping over committee members. fastatomstruct also supports several models with shared neighbors and exports member forces and variances. We therefore compare to the native **multi-model** calculator and separately to an E/F-only loop that already shares the exact topology. Only the latter comparison isolates the additional feature sharing. On the pinned MACE 0.3.16 stack the native calculator also computes stress. [MACE source](https://github.com/ACEsuit/mace/blob/v0.3.16/mace/calculators/mace.py) · [fastatomstruct](https://ag-salinga.zivgitlabpages.uni-muenster.de/fastatomstruct/calculators/mace.html)

LAMMPS ML-IAP is a relevant production control for distributed MD, GPU tensor products and atomic virials. It requires a converted checkpoint and a compatible LAMMPS build. A comparison against native PyTorch/ASE does not establish an advantage over ML-IAP, Symmetrix-XL or FastMACE. [ML-IAP integration](https://mace-docs.readthedocs.io/en/latest/guide/lammps_mliap.html)

## Experiments worth doing next

1. **Reduce force-evaluation time.** Profile a trained checkpoint with the source and destination workloads held fixed. Prioritize the largest interaction/product contractions or their force derivatives. Compare E/F-only matched backends, then remeasure moving MD, including neighbor rebuilds.
2. **Reduce committee cost while preserving disagreement.** The first comparison reuses physical edges. The second also shares invariant geometric inputs to independent networks. When this saves little, the next candidate is batched execution of compatible learned interaction operators across checkpoints. Each member keeps its own weights and gradient. Quantify launch savings and peak memory for 2, 3 and 5 members before writing a specialized kernel.
3. **Test transfer to application models.** Benchmark a liquid interface and a solid of comparable size, with a stock MACE checkpoint first. A conditional charge or magnetic model needs a separate adapter and matching reference observables before performance numbers transfer. For replica or umbrella sampling, report aggregate trajectory throughput and the latency of one trajectory separately.

## Related implementations for the derivative experiments

- An's *FlashSC* specializes the product blocks of a frozen MACE checkpoint. Its 2026 thesis describes a CUDA executor, backward handling, and end-to-end LAMMPS timing. The paired `both` and `both_deferred` trials in this repository instead compare two derivative schedules using exactly the same coefficient table and monomial ordering. [Thesis](https://www.dcollection.net/handler/ajou/000000036335) · [Measurements](../benchmarks/adjoints.md)
- JET-MLIPs optimizes radial filters and edge convolution. Table 2 reports a 5.9× speedup for MACE-MP-0 Medium versus ML-IAP with cuEquivariance on an H200 under the authors' benchmark conditions. Those tests use a different GPU and precision than our V100 trials. [Yoo and Ju, 2026 preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.31865)
- Sphericart and EquiTriton already provide analytic Cartesian spherical harmonics and their derivatives. Timing a full moving trajectory measures how much of a kernel-level gain survives the rest of force evaluation. [Sphericart](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0156307) · [EquiTriton](https://openreview.net/forum?id=ftK00FO5wq)
- PyG SchNet uses scalar continuous-filter messages driven by interatomic distances. SchNetPack PaiNN additionally uses edge directions to update vector features. The shared Cartesian derivative accepts either output while each framework supplies its own pretrained interaction and readout blocks. [SchNet](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2017/hash/303ed4c69846ab36c2904d3ba8573050-Abstract.html) · [PaiNN](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v139/schutt21a.html) · [Matched molecular benchmarks](../benchmarks/portable.md)
